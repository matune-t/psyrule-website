2. What PsyRule does

  • Reads your trading-platform data via a read-only API token you provide
  • Stores your trade history and your pre-session check-in answers
  • Names behavioral patterns it detects in your history (e.g. "your revenge loop")
  • Surfaces an intervention card during a session when one of those patterns starts to run, with the historical cost of that pattern for you
  • Sends a weekly behavioral report by email
  • Lets you create personal rules and tracks rule breaks

3. What PsyRule does not do

  • It does not place, modify, or close trades on your behalf
  • It does not provide trading, financial, or investment advice
  • It does not guarantee any outcome — passing a challenge, profitability, or improvement
  • It does not store your trading-platform password (we use a read-only API token you generate)
  • It does not share your data with prop firms, brokers, or marketers (see Privacy)

Modeled cost figures shown on this site and in the product (e.g. "~$312 per event") are derived from a small private beta and internal historical data. They are illustrative — your actual numbers depend on your own trading history.
